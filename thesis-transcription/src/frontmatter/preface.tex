\textbf{PREFACE}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.8in}

Many people have helped to make my graduate experience possible, productive,
and enjoyable. I would like to acknowledge them publicly. Foremost is my
advisor, Klaus Berkling. He has been more than a teacher, he has been my
friend. I appreciate the freedom he gave me to explore my own ideas and the
fact that he never said ``I told you so'' when an idea flopped. More than
anything else, Klaus Berkling has taught me that conventional wisdom is not
always wise.

My life as a graduate student would not have been nearly so productive or
enjoyable without the financial support of the CASE Center and the School of
Computer and Information Sciences. On behalf of myself and my family, I would
like to thank Brad Strait and Dean Gideon Frieder, respectively.

Alan Robinson was always available for sounding out an idea, filling in gaps
in my understanding, and helping me find gainful employment after graduate
school. He has been an inspiration and role model. Damir Jamsek and Paul
Hummen provided lots of stimulating conversation and insight into declarative
programming. Damir helped with the proofs in Chapter 4 and the analysis of
WINTER in Chapter 5.

Finally, I wish to thank my wife for her unwavering patience, support, and
faith in me.
